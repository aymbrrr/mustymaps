musty's map.
rate where you eat, study & lock in @ cal poly slo.
onboarding
get in fast.
One intro, then sign up with a verified @calpoly.edu email and a 6-digit code, or sign in if you already have an account.
feed
what’s good rn.
Trending spots ranked by live check-ins, then every spot with its busyness, overall rating and top comment.
map
tap a pin, get the vibe.
Restaurants and study spots on a real campus map. Tapping a pin expands a bar with rating, live busyness and quick actions.
location
the full picture.
Ratings, a breakdown by category, reviews, and how busy it is at any hour on any day.
rate a spot
say it in 30 sec.
Overall stars, category details, a busyness check-in, tags, notes and a photo.
leaderboard
top raters.
Rank by ratings, reviews, spots or streak, all-time or this week. You can always see your rank and how far you are from the next person.
profiles
you & everyone else.
Your profile lists every rating with edit and share. Public profiles show how someone rates and where they go.
settings
all the toggles.
Notifications with mute all, location, privacy and tracking, accessibility, legal, and account deletion. Covers everything Apple requires.
