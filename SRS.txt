1. INTRODUCTION
* PROBLEM STATEMENT: WHAT SPECIFIC PROBLEM ARE YOU SOLVING?
Cal Poly students often have to walk to dining areas, study spaces, and
other campus locations without knowing how crowded they are, how long the wait will be, or
whether useful resources such as leftover food are available.
* TARGET AUDIENCE: WHO ARE THE STAKEHOLDERS?
Cal Poly students who want real-time information about campus locations.
* SCOPE: WHAT IS THE SCOPE OF THIS PROJECT?
Musty Map will provide a crowdsourced campus map where students can view and
submit information such as crowd levels, estimated wait times, photos, ratings, and leftover food
updates. Users will also be able to create profiles, favorite locations, and flag inaccurate or
inappropriate content.


2. USER STORIES and Product Vision
Product Vision
Product Vision: For Cal Poly students who need up-to-date information about busy campus
locations, Musty Map is a crowdsourced campus map application that provides real-time
information about crowd levels, wait times, leftover food, and other useful campus updates.
Unlike general map applications, Musty Map focuses specifically on Cal Poly and uses real-time
reports from students to help users make better decisions about where and when to go.
